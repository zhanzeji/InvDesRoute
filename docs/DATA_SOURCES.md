# Sources and redistribution scope

Materials Project: https://materialsproject.org/ . Structures and source thermodynamic records remain under the provider's applicable terms; entry-level license/version metadata are not uniformly present in the archived inputs.

Literature sources: Kononova et al., https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-019-0224-1 and https://github.com/CederGroupHub/text-mined-synthesis_public ; Lee et al., https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-06222-y and https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30423274 . Public recipe access does not by itself establish a blanket license for every raw field or later snapshot.

Thermal source: NIST-JANAF, https://janaf.nist.gov/ , https://doi.org/10.18434/T42S31 . Raw downloaded tables are excluded from these public artifacts.

The data release contains author-generated score features, residuals, frozen evaluation metrics and numerical summaries. It does not redistribute raw CIF/CONTCAR files, source MP energy tables, source recipe JSON/full text, raw JANAF tables or model weights. The CC BY 4.0 license continues the existing data-record license for the released author-generated snapshot and does not relicense excluded third-party sources. Source-dependent inputs are held in a separate, explicitly non-public archive pending source-specific redistribution review.
